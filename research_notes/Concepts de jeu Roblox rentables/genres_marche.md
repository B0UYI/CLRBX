# Roblox game market by genre, late 2026 (as of 18 Nov 2026)

Method note: WebFetch could not reach most hosts in this session (sec.gov, rowatcher.com, bloxodes.com blocked by network policy), so every figure below comes from search-result snippets, not full-page reads. Primary-source figures (Roblox 8-K/10-Q) are labelled; all genre and per-game revenue numbers are third-party estimates (Roblox publishes no per-genre revenue). Trackers use different snapshot times and models and often disagree.

## 1. Which genres lead in revenue, CCU, playtime and retention in 2026?

### Takeaway
Simulation (incl. idle, garden, "steal" and pet/egg sims) leads on both live players (about 36% of players on one tracker) and total estimated revenue. It is also the most concentrated genre. Per-game median revenue is higher in Sports & Racing, Roleplay and Shooter. Rivals (shooter), Blox Fruits (anime RPG) and Steal An Egg (steal-sim) top most 2026 earnings estimates.

### Cited Findings
**Genre share of players (live snapshots, Nov 2026)**
- Simulation is about 36% of players. Survival grew most week over week (+13%) and Puzzle shrank most. — [Statroblox, most popular genres (updated ~Nov 2026)](https://statroblox.com/guides/most-popular-roblox-genres)
- Bloxodes live genre ranking: 1 Simulation, 2 Survival, 3 Roleplay & Avatar Sim, 4 Action, 5 Shooter (undated snapshot). — [Bloxodes stats](https://bloxodes.com/stats)
- VEXU July 2026 report: Simulation first by live players, then Roleplay & Avatar Sim, then Survival. — [VEXU July 2026](https://www.vexu.gg/reports/roblox-statistics-july-2026)
- From 2021 to mid-2025, Roleplay/avatar experiences had the most daily visits, with Simulation close behind (2025 data). — [games.gg](https://games.gg/news/roblox-growth-and-player-trends/)

**Genre revenue (estimates)**
- profitable.app (Nov 2026) ranks Simulation as the highest-grossing genre by total estimated revenue. It cautions that totals reward genres with many games and that median visits per game is the better metric for a newcomer. — [profitable.app genres](https://profitable.app/roblox/genres)
- Median monthly revenue per game by genre: Sports & Racing about $1,084, Roleplay & Avatar Sim about $1,018, Shooter about $943. — [profitable.app stats (Nov 2026)](https://profitable.app/roblox/stats)
- Average monthly revenue for top-100 games: Simulator $6,800 vs Roleplay $4,200. Simulators score worse on CCU and retention. — [RoLearn Roleplay vs Simulator 2026](https://rolearn.dev/trend-reports/roleplay-vs-simulator-genre-comparison-2026/)
- Median visits per day by genre (BloxBunny): Social about 28,813, RPG about 26,793, Simulation about 8,438 with the largest game count (2,063). Simulation's low median reflects heavy competition. — [BloxBunny calculator](https://bloxbunny.com/roblox-revenue-calculator), via search snippet
- Simulators are "still the most profitable genre but the most concentrated". In most subgenres the top 2 games control more than 80% of revenue. — [RoWatcher Genre Opportunity Map 2026](https://rowatcher.com/news/roblox-genre-opportunity-map-2026-where-smart-developers-are-building-next)

**Top-earning games 2026 (estimates; trackers conflict)**
- RoWatcher, Q1 2026: Rivals $8–15M+/month (#1), Blox Fruits $8–14M, Pet Simulator 99 $4–8M, Steal a Brainrot $3–7M. Brookhaven, Adopt Me! and PS99 are also in the top 10. Rivals topped PC and mobile revenue rankings in Jan 2026. — [RoWatcher top 10 earners 2026](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-10-highest-earning-roblox-games-in-2026-and-what-they-mean-for-the-platform)
- Nov 2026: Roblox's Top Earning chart reads RIVALS #1, then Steal An Egg, then Blox Fruits. — [Statroblox chart](https://statroblox.com/leaderboards/chart)
- Another Nov 2026 tracker ranks Anime Adventures, RIVALS and Blox Fruits as the top 3: Blox Fruits about $4.35M over 30 days, Rivals about $1.76M. — [Bloxrant](https://bloxrant.com/roblox-top-earning-games)
- BloxBunny's model: Steal An Egg $3.2M/month, Rivals $796.5K, Blox Fruits $732.9K. These differ by about 10x from RoWatcher, which shows how unreliable the models are. — [BloxBunny top earners](https://bloxbunny.com/top-earning-games)
- Grow a Garden is estimated at about $1.75M/month (confidence 0.81). Vacuum For Brainrots! is about $33.6K/month. — [RoWatcher Grow a Garden](https://rowatcher.com/games/7436755782/grow-a-garden); [RoWatcher Vacuum For Brainrots](https://rowatcher.com/games/9846315160/vacuum-for-brainrots)
- One Aug 2026 post claims anime fighter Gakuran was the #1 earner on one day. This conflicts with the other trackers. — via [Bloxrant search snippet](https://bloxrant.com/roblox-top-earning-games)
- Most-played games are not the top earners: playtime and revenue rankings diverge. — [MaxPowerGaming](https://www.maxpowergaming.co/post/roblox-s-most-played-games-aren-t-its-top-earners)

**Playtime**
- Roblox's top 1,000 experiences account for roughly 80% of in-experience hours (2024 and 2025, from the 10-K, cited by Naavik). — [Naavik State of UGC 2026](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/the-state-of-ugc-games-2026/)
- Roblox was the most-played game by total hours in H1 2026. Older players (Newzoo panel) logged 4.05B hours in H1 2026. — [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/other/item-52471); [tech-insider](https://tech-insider.org/roblox-older-players-4-05-billion-hours-newzoo-2026/)

### Inferences
- Simulation is where the money and the players are, but outcomes are winner-take-most. For a newcomer, per-game medians favour Sports/Racing, Roleplay and Shooter.
- Shooter (Rivals) and anime RPG/fighters (Blox Fruits, Anime Adventures, Gakuran) earn much more per player than they rank by CCU. Older and PC players likely monetize these better.

### Gaps
- No primary or official genre-level revenue, retention or D1/D7 data exists. Roblox does not disclose it.
- profitable.app's median visits per game figures and its full genre table could not be loaded.
- No genre-level retention numbers were found, only qualitative claims that simulators retain worse.

## 2. Which genres are saturated and which are growing fastest?

### Takeaway
Basic simulators and obbies are saturated or declining: there are many launches and CCU per game is falling. Survival and horror, "steal"/raid social-sims (brainrot to egg), social-idle hybrids, competitive FPS and IP crossovers (K-Pop Demon Hunters) are growing. Each new viral format is cloned within weeks.

### Cited Findings
- Simulator launches reached 189 in Q4 2025, while average CCU per game fell 20% in early 2026. Only the top handful of established simulators are expected to keep performing. New entrants without a novel mechanic face "near-impossible odds". — [RoLearn genre predictions 2026](https://rolearn.dev/trend-reports/genre-shift-2026-predictions/), via search snippet
- RoLearn rates obbies as declining since mid-2024, with the decline expected to continue through 2026. Basic simulators are also "declining". Its favoured categories are social-idle hybrids and competitive FPS. — [RoLearn genre predictions 2026](https://rolearn.dev/trend-reports/genre-shift-2026-predictions/)
- Idle and garden-themed experiences were "the defining genre story of early 2026", led by Grow a Garden. — [RoLearn Q1 2026 market trends](https://rolearn.dev/trend-reports/q1-2026-market-trends)
- Survival was the fastest-growing genre week over week (+13%) on Statroblox's Nov 2026 snapshot. — [Statroblox](https://statroblox.com/guides/most-popular-roblox-genres)
- Fish It! is described as a fishing sim "in one of Roblox's most-cloned genres" (~143K playing). Sell Lemons is a viral idle/tycoon breakout (~135K). — [RoWatcher most popular games](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-most-popular-roblox-games-right-now-ranked-by-live-players)
- Clone wave around brainrot: Escape Tsunami For Brainrots!, Brainrot Tower Defense, Vacuum For Brainrots! and others. — [eGamersWorld brainrot games](https://egamersworld.com/blog/best-roblox-brainrot-games-RAWgOGXr40); [RoWatcher Vacuum For Brainrots](https://rowatcher.com/games/9846315160/vacuum-for-brainrots)
- Week of Sep 28 – Oct 4, 2026: "CCU Surges 39% as Egg Games and K-Pop Demons Rule the Week". — [RoWatcher W40](https://rowatcher.com/news/roblox-week-in-numbers-2026-w40)
- Naavik: Roblox is "modestly expanding in genre diversity" and now hosts AAA-style shooters, RPGs and sports-action. — [Naavik State of UGC 2026](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/the-state-of-ugc-games-2026/)
- More than 80% of the top 100 games by hours used AI tools in the past month, according to the Q3 2026 shareholder letter as reported by press. This lowers the cost of cloning. — [RoWatcher W44](https://rowatcher.com/news/roblox-week-in-numbers-2026-w44) / [MassivelyOP](https://massivelyop.com/2026/10/30/roblox-marks-a-rise-in-revenue-more-gen-ai-games-and-a-chase-for-over-18-users-in-third-quarter-earnings-report/)

### Inferences
- The "steal X" format went from Steal a Brainrot (2025) to Steal An Egg (Jul 2026) to K-Pop egg hatchers. Formats are refreshed by swapping the collectible or theme, so the theme saturates fast but the mechanic (base-building, raiding, rarity gacha) persists.
- AI-assisted development and clone speed mean the window for a trend may be only weeks. Launching early on a theme matters more than polish in sim/idle.

### Gaps
- No hard count of games per genre over time, or CCU per game by genre for 2026, beyond RoLearn's simulator figure (not verified at source).
- No data was found on saturation of "+1 Speed" incremental games or egg games specifically.

## 3. Cultural and aesthetic trends driving breakout hits; top breakout games Sep–Nov 2026

### Takeaway
The dominant late-2026 breakout is Steal An Egg: created 25 Jul 2026, peaking around 12–14M CCU in Sep–Oct 2026, and still #1 weekly in mid-Nov. Other drivers are brainrot (Italian-brainrot meme) carry-overs, the K-Pop Demon Hunters (Netflix IP) wave, horror/anomaly and co-op survival (Animal Hospital, 99 Nights in the Forest, Forsaken, Dandy's World), cozy garden/idle, and anime fighters/RPGs. The Roblox Halloween event ran 18 Oct – 16 Nov.

### Cited Findings
**Steal An Egg (Misfits Gaming portfolio)**

- Peak CCU readings: RoStats 14,313,917 on 19 Sep 2026 (its biggest day was 12 Sep, going from 1.68M to 3.32M). Aream & Co. Q3 report: 14.2M. RBLXSTAT: 12,443,225 on 3 Oct. rblxdb: 12,667,024. It has reached about 6B visits. — [RoStats records](https://rostats.io/records); [RBLXSTAT](https://rblxstat.com/games/10563114921); [rblxdb](https://rblxdb.com/records); [OtakuKart](https://otakukart.com/robloxs-steal-an-egg-reaches-6-billion-visits-and-14-2-million-peak-concurrent-players/)

## 4. Concentration of earnings (top 10 vs median; DevEx totals)

### Takeaway
DevEx totals grew to about $1.5B+ in calendar 2025 and about $1.7B for Jul 2025 – Jun 2026, but they are extremely concentrated. The top 10 averaged about $65.7M each, while the median DevEx participant (of more than 42,000) earned about $1,500. Q3 2026 DevEx fees fell 6% YoY to $403M.

### Cited Findings
- Roblox's annual economic report (Jul 2025 – Jun 2026): about $1.7B DevEx (+~50% YoY). Top 10 averaged $65.7M (vs $33.9M in the prior report), top 100 $10.8M, top 1,000 $1.4M. More than 42,000 DevEx participants, median about $1,500. — [TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/113585/robloxs-top-10-creators-averaged-dollars65-7-million-each-while-the-median-made-dollars1500-and-roblox-everywhere-is-the-fix/index.html); [TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/113855-roblox-top-creators-making-65-million-year-while.html); [ixbt (Sep 2026)](https://ixbt.games/en/news/2026/09/14/435627-roblox-raskryla-doxody-sozdatelei-kontenta.html)
- Calendar 2025 (Roblox/Q4 2025 disclosures): more than $1.5B DevEx (+63% YoY, first year above $1B). Top 1,000 averaged about $1.3M (+50%+). Q4 2025 alone was a record $477M (+70%). — [Lindsey Gamble on Threads summarizing Roblox](https://www.threads.com/@lindseygamble_/post/DUf3DVrkwJ5/in-creators-have-earned-over-billion-through-robloxs-developer-exchange-dev-ex); [Q4 2025 shareholder letter](https://s27.q4cdn.com/984876518/files/doc_financials/2025/q4/Q4-2025-Shareholder-Letter.pdf)
- The earlier report's $33.9M top-10 average applied to a window of either Mar 2024 – Mar 2025 or the year to Sep 2025 (**sources conflict**). — [Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/amp/story/gaming/gaming-news/robloxs-top-developers-average-339-million-in-annual-earnings); [SQ Magazine](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/roblox-game-creation-and-monetization-statistics/)
- Q3 2026 developer exchange fees were $403M, down 6% YoY, due to lower bookings, partly offset by an 8.5% increase in the DevEx rate. — [Roblox 10-Q Q3 2026 (SEC)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026068253/rblx-20260930.htm), via search snippet. **Conflict:** one press summary gives $363M. — [gaming.net](https://gaming.net/roblox-q3-revenue-rises-16-as-bookings-fall-12-to-1-7-billion)
- Naavik: UGC payouts across Roblox, Fortnite and Overwolf were about $2.2B in 2025 (+47%). Growth in engagement-based payouts slowed (about +5% YoY, estimate). — [Naavik State of UGC 2026](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/the-state-of-ugc-games-2026/)
- More than 5,000 games have over 1M visits. GEEIQ counts about 6.7M active experiences. — [Wikipedia List of Roblox games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Roblox_games); [GameDev Reports / Newzoo & GEEIQ](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/newzoo-and-geeiq-the-roblox-audience)

### Inferences
- The top-10 vs median ratio is about 44,000:1. Combined with the top 1,000 experiences holding about 80% of hours, success is a power law. The realistic planning case for a small studio is the median or low thousands of dollars per year, unless it rides an early trend.
- DevEx is falling in H2 2026 (Q3 -6%), while 2025 grew +63%. The pie is shrinking short-term, even with the higher DevEx rate.

### Gaps
- No official 2026 year-to-date DevEx total. The Q3 regional and creator split is not available.

## 5. Platform stats (latest: Q3 2026, reported 29–30 Oct 2026)

### Takeaway
Q3 2026 was the first YoY user decline. DAU was 138M (-9% YoY, +12% QoQ), hours about 34B (-15% YoY), bookings $1.697B (-12%), revenue $1.57B (+16%). The causes were age-check friction and players shifting to less-monetized experiences. Adults are a growing share: 18+ are 29% of age-checked DAU globally and 37% in the US. The audience is about 80% phone.

### Cited Findings
- Q3 2026: DAU 138M (-9% YoY; +12% QoQ), hours about 34B (-15% YoY; +17% QoQ). These are consistent, since Q2 was a trough. Bookings $1.697B vs $1.922B (-12%), but above guidance. Revenue about $1.572B (+16%). Net loss about $230M. MUP 30.3M (-16%). ABPDAU $12.29 (-3%). — [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/finance/item-52549); [gaming.net](https://gaming.net/roblox-q3-revenue-rises-16-as-bookings-fall-12-to-1-7-billion); [GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/roblox-daily-players-fall-q3-2026/); [Q3 2026 shareholder letter (PDF)](https://s27.q4cdn.com/984876518/files/content_files/Q3-2026-Earnings-Shareholder-Letter.pdf). **Conflict:** one summary gives hours as 29B; the letter says nearly 34B.
- Q3 2026 bookings by region (Investing.com, not verified in the filing): US & Canada $907M (-13%), Europe $360M (-12%), APAC $226M (-7%), Rest of World $204M (-8%). — [Investing.com slides](https://au.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/roblox-q3-2026-slides-first-user-decline-marks-inflection-point-93CH-4727761)
- Q4 2026 guidance: bookings $1.89–2.00B (-10% to -15% YoY), revenue +4–12%. The stock fell about 16% after the report. FY2026 revenue growth outlook was trimmed to 20–25%. — [WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/finance/item-52549); [247wallst](https://247wallst.com/companies/rblx/earnings/2026/Q1)

US 18–34 DAU grew +26% and hours +23%. Users 18+ monetize at least 50% more than under-18s. — [Q3 2026 shareholder letter](https://s27.q4cdn.com/984876518/files/content_files/Q3-2026-Earnings-Shareholder-Letter.pdf); [Benzinga transcript](https://www.benzinga.com/news/26/10/62722787/roblox-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript); [Motley Fool transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/30/roblox-rblx-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)

- Q4 2025 hours were 36B (+88% YoY), the 2025 peak. — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/the-state-of-ugc-games-2026/)

### Inferences
- The platform is contracting in H2 2026 versus the 2025 boom (Grow a Garden and Steal a Brainrot era). The pie per game is smaller, and monetization per hour is under pressure among young users.
- Growth is in adult and older users (higher-value genres: shooter, RPG, competitive) and in India and Japan (anime affinity, low ARPU in India).
- A phone-first UI is mandatory (about 80% phone).

### Gaps
- An exact mobile share of DAU or bookings from Roblox itself was not found.
- The full Q3 2026 regional DAU table was not retrieved (only fragments). The US/UK hours growth fragments ("+55% and 63%") could not be attributed.
- Q3 2026 18+ global share as a share of all DAU (not just age-checked) is not available.
